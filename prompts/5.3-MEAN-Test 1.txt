### Problem Statement
A customer service call center reports that the length of customer calls has a mean of 8.5 minutes and a standard deviation of 4.2 minutes. The distribution of call lengths is skewed to the right. A supervisor randomly selects 49 calls. Find the probability that the mean call length in the sample is less than 7.8 minutes. Show all steps using the SPDC structure.

### Model Solution
S – State.
1. Population parameter: μ = the true mean length of all customer calls to the call center.
2. Sample statistic: x̄ = the mean length of the 49 randomly selected calls.
3. n = 49 is the sample size, the number of calls randomly selected by the supervisor.
4. 8.5 minutes is the population mean (μ = 8.5) and 4.2 minutes is the population standard deviation (σ = 4.2); the population distribution is skewed to the right.
5. Problem: Find P(x̄ < 7.8).
P – Plan.
1. Randomness: the 49 calls were randomly selected, so x̄ is an unbiased estimator of μ and we can generalize to all calls to the call center.
2. 10% condition: there must be at least 10(49) = 490 calls; the call center receives far more than 490 calls, so n < 10% of N and the observations are approximately independent.
3. Normality: the population is skewed right, but n = 49 ≥ 30, so by the Central Limit Theorem the sampling distribution of x̄ is approximately Normal.
4. Mean: μ_x̄ = μ = 8.5.
5. Standard deviation: σ_x̄ = σ/√n = 4.2/√49 = 0.6. Normal model: x̄ ~ N(8.5, 0.6).
D – Do.
1. Sampling distribution: x̄ ~ N(8.5, 0.6).
2. P(x̄ < 7.8)
3. = P[Z < (7.8 − 8.5)/0.6]
4. = P(Z < −1.17)
5. = 0.1210
C – Conclude.
• There is about a 12% probability that, in a random sample of 49 calls to the call center, the sample mean call length will be less than 7.8 minutes.
