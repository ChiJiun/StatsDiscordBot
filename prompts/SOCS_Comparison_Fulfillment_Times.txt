An online retailer ships customer orders from two distribution centers. For each day, the company calculates the average order fulfillment time, defined as the number of hours from order placement to shipment. The histograms show the daily average fulfillment times for 48 randomly selected days at the Taichung center and 44 randomly selected days at the Kaohsiung center in 2026.

Summary statistics are calculated from the raw data, in hours. Extreme values: Taichung — two smallest 23.0, 23.3; two largest 29.1, 29.4. Kaohsiung — two smallest 23.4, 23.8; three largest 31.9, 32.8, 33.6.
Task (20 points): Compare the distributions of daily average order fulfillment times at the Taichung and Kaohsiung distribution centers. Address shape, outliers, center, and spread, justify your answers with appropriate calculations, and interpret your results in context.

### Model Solution
The daily average order fulfillment times on 48 days at the Taichung center and 44 days at the Kaohsiung center in 2026 differ in shape. Both distributions are unimodal, but Taichung’s distribution is roughly symmetric, with its mean (26.19 hours) close to its median (26.2 hours), while Kaohsiung’s distribution is skewed to the right, with a long right tail and a mean (26.89 hours) greater than its median (26.2 hours).
Using the 1.5 × IQR rule, Taichung’s fences are 25.2 − 1.5(1.8) = 22.5 and 27.0 + 1.5(1.8) = 29.7. Since all Taichung times are between 23.0 and 29.4 hours, Taichung has no outliers. Kaohsiung’s fences are 25.0 − 1.5(3.0) = 20.5 and 28.0 + 1.5(3.0) = 32.5, so the times of 32.8 and 33.6 hours are high outliers, while 31.9 hours is not an outlier.
Because Kaohsiung’s distribution is skewed and has outliers, the median is the better measure of center. The medians are the same: 26.2 hours at both centers. Kaohsiung’s higher mean is caused by its right tail and outliers, so comparing means would wrongly suggest that orders at Kaohsiung typically take longer to ship.
For the same reason, the IQR is the better measure of spread. Kaohsiung is more variable than Taichung: its IQR is 3.0 hours, compared with 1.8 hours for Taichung. On the middle 50% of days, orders at Kaohsiung took between 25.0 and 28.0 hours to ship, while orders at Taichung took between 25.2 and 27.0 hours. In other words, on a typical day, orders at both centers ship about 26.2 hours after they are placed, but Kaohsiung’s fulfillment times are much less predictable from day to day, and on a few days they were unusually slow.
