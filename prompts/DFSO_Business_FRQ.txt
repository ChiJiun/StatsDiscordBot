### Problem Statement
Describing a Scatterplot (DFSO) 
Context: Advertising and Sales
A marketing manager at a retail company wants to investigate the relationship between weekly advertising spending and weekly sales revenue. The manager randomly selects 20 weeks from the company’s records and creates the scatterplot below.
Question
Describe the relationship between weekly advertising spending and weekly sales revenue. Be sure to discuss the direction, form, strength, and any unusual features of the relationship.
 
Figure 1. Weekly advertising spending and weekly sales revenue for 20 randomly selected weeks.

### Model Solution
There is a positive, roughly linear, moderately strong relationship between weekly advertising spending and weekly sales revenue. In general, weeks with higher advertising spending tend to have higher sales revenue. There is one possible outlier at about $20,000 in advertising spending and $58,000 in sales revenue; this week has substantially lower sales revenue than would be expected from the overall pattern.
